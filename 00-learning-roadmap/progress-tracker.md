## Current Lesson Record

### Milestone 1 --- Understanding Logic

**Lesson 1: What Is Logic?**

Status: In Progress

The lesson introduced logic as using information and reasoning to reach
a conclusion. It presented three basic building blocks:

1.  **Information** --- what we know.
2.  **Rule or condition** --- what determines what is allowed or what
    should happen.
3.  **Conclusion** --- what follows from the information and rule.

### First Exercise: Bookshop Stock

**Situation**

A bookshop has five copies of a particular book in stock. A customer
wants to buy two copies.

**Rule**

A customer can only buy a quantity that is available in stock.

**My task**

-   Decide whether the bookshop should allow or reject the request.
-   Identify the information given.
-   Identify the rule.
-   Explain the conclusion that follows.

**Exercise status:** Awaiting my attempt.

**Result / feedback:** To be completed after the exercise is attempted
and reviewed.

-   Change a lesson to **Completed** only after its learning work is
    finished.
-   Change an exercise to **Completed** after attempting it and
    reviewing the feedback.
-   Record evidence of skills demonstrated.
-   Update milestone status after the milestone assessment.
-   Keep unfinished work visible; do not mark work complete just to make
    the tracker look full.

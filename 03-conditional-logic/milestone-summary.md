# Milestone 3 — Conditional Logic

**Course:** Reasoning to Code  
**Milestone:** 3 of 8  
**Status:** Learning and assessment completed  
**Assessment:** 30 questions — 30 correct

## Purpose

This milestone develops the ability to translate written rules into logical conditions and express them in PHP. It covers conditional direction, necessary and sufficient conditions, valid reasoning, logical operators, PHP conditionals, comparison operators, boundaries, and requirement language.

## Learning outcomes

By completing this milestone, the learner can:

- Translate `if`, `only if`, `unless`, `whenever`, `provided that`, and `if and only if` statements.
- Distinguish necessary from sufficient conditions.
- Apply modus ponens and modus tollens, and recognise affirming the consequent and denying the antecedent.
- Combine conditions with AND, OR and NOT.
- Use parentheses and understand PHP operator precedence and short-circuit evaluation.
- Write and trace PHP `if`, `elseif`, `else`, and nested conditions.
- Choose comparison operators correctly, including strict comparisons and boundary conditions.
- Distinguish `can`, `must`, `shall`, `should`, `may`, and `will` in requirements.
- Understand foundational testing concepts introduced alongside conditional logic.

# Milestone 1: Understanding Logic

**Status: Completed**

## Milestone purpose
Build the foundation for careful reasoning by learning to distinguish information, evidence, assumptions, and conclusions.

## Concepts covered
- Thinking and reasoning
- Logic as careful examination of information and connections between ideas
- Facts and assumptions
- Evidence and its limits
- Observation
- Inference
- Conclusions
- Possibilities versus established explanations

## Assessment record
- Initial knowledge checks: 8/8 correct.
- Milestone review questions: 8/8 correct.
- Final practical clinic-booking reasoning assessment: completed successfully, with some instructor guidance.

## Practical application
The scenario involved a patient who reported a 10 AM appointment that was missing from the application's current appointment list.

The learner recognised that the screenshot was evidence that the appointment had been displayed, but did not by itself prove that the appointment had been persisted in the database. The original database record was later found with status `cancelled`. The reason and timing of cancellation were not established by those facts alone. The cancellation history or audit log was identified as a sensible next investigation.

## Demonstrated progress
The learner showed an ability to distinguish evidence from assumptions, identify unknown information, and avoid treating an unverified explanation as a fact.

Some parts of the practical investigation were completed with instructor guidance. The next milestone will continue strengthening independent reasoning.

## What comes next
**Milestone 2: Building Reasoning Ability**

The next milestone will develop the ability to examine premises and conclusions, recognise valid and invalid reasoning, and identify contradictions and common reasoning errors.

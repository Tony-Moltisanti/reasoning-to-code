# Lesson 1: Facts, Assumptions, and Evidence

## Facts
A **fact** is a claim that is actually true and can be verified. A statement is not automatically a fact just because someone says it confidently.

## Assumptions
An **assumption** is something treated as true without having verified it sufficiently.

Example: A patient says, "I booked an appointment for 10 AM." Until the booking records are checked, we should not assume the appointment was successfully saved.

## Evidence
**Evidence** is information relevant to evaluating a claim. Evidence may support or challenge a claim. Its quality and reliability matter, and it does not always prove a claim on its own.

A screenshot showing an appointment at 10 AM is evidence that the booking information was displayed on the patient's screen. By itself, it may not prove that the booking was successfully saved in the application's database.

## Keep the ideas separate
- A claim is something being said or proposed.
- Evidence is information that helps us evaluate the claim.
- An assumption is an unverified idea being treated as true.
- A fact is something that is actually true and can be verified.

## Key takeaway
Treat claims carefully. Ask what evidence supports them and what still needs to be checked.

# Lesson 2: Invalid Reasoning and Counterexamples

Reasoning is **invalid** when its conclusion does not necessarily follow from its premises.

Example: All nurses work in healthcare. David works in healthcare. Therefore, David is a nurse. David could be a doctor, pharmacist or another healthcare professional. The conclusion does not follow.

## The counterexample test
1. Temporarily accept the premises as true.
2. Imagine a situation where those premises remain true.
3. Ask whether the conclusion could still be false.

If yes, the argument is invalid. A failed payment may still have a transaction reference, so having a reference does not prove that payment succeeded.

## Necessary conditions
A necessary condition must be present for something else to hold. If every confirmed appointment must have a patient ID, an appointment without one cannot be confirmed. But having an ID alone does not prove that an appointment is confirmed.

**Software connection:** Test pending, cancelled, failed, duplicate and interrupted scenarios, not just successful ones.

# Lesson 3: Contradictions and Context

A **contradiction** occurs when two statements cannot both be true under the same conditions. An appointment cannot be both confirmed and not confirmed at the same time if only one current status is allowed.

Context matters. Sarah's appointment confirmed at 9 AM and cancelled at 10 AM can both be true because the status changed over time.

If two current records say the same appointment is both `confirmed` and `cancelled` at 9 AM, that is an inconsistency requiring investigation. It does not establish who caused it or why.

Investigate appointment history, audit logs, database updates, application logic and concurrent operations. These are investigation paths, not proven causes.
